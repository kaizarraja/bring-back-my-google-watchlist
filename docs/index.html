Bring Back My Google Watchlist
Install FAQ GitHub

Google's Watchlist button keeps disappearing. This brings it back.

A free browser extension. Search any movie or show on Google and press Add to watchlist. Search my watchlist and your list is right there, just like before.

Download the extension How to install

Free and open source · Chrome, Brave and Edge on a computer ·

What it does

Save from Google

An “Add to watchlist” button on every movie and TV page in Google Search, with the poster saved too.

Search “my watchlist”

Your list shows at the top of the results, with To watch / Watched, Movies / TV filters and search. “my watchlist movies” works too.

Same list everywhere

Sign in with Google once per browser and your list syncs between Chrome, Brave, Edge and your other computers.

Install (about 2 minutes)

The extension isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so it's installed by hand. It's a one-time setup.

  1. Download bring-back-my-google-watchlist.zip and double-click it to unzip. You get a folder called bring-back-my-google-watchlist.
  2. Move that folder somewhere permanent, like your Documents folder. The browser runs the extension from it, so don't delete it later.
  3. Open the extensions page: type chrome://extensions in the address bar (Brave: brave://extensions, Edge: edge://extensions).
  4. Turn on Developer mode with the switch in the top-right corner (in Edge it's on the left).
  5. Click “Load unpacked” and choose the bring-back-my-google-watchlist folder.
  6. Pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Bring Back My Google Watchlist.

Chrome may show a “Disable developer mode extensions” message when it starts. That's normal for extensions installed by hand. Just close it, and don't choose Disable.

Then try it

  1. Search Google for a movie, like Inception, and press Add to watchlist.
  2. Search my watchlist.
  3. Optional: click the toolbar icon and Sign in with Google to sync to use the same list in other browsers.

Bring over your old Google watchlist

  1. Click the toolbar icon, then Import from Google. This opens your saved items on Google.
  2. Open your watchlist collection (often called Search Watchlist or Google TV watchlist) and scroll to the bottom so every title loads.
  3. Press Import to My Watchlist in the bottom-right corner. Missing posters and years fill in over the next few times you search “my watchlist”.

Updating

When a new version is out, the toolbar window tells you. Download the new zip, replace the old bring-back-my-google-watchlist folder with the new one, then press the reload arrow on the extension's card in the extensions page. Your list stays.

Questions

Is this made by Google?

No. It's an independent, free project that works on Google Search pages. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Does it change my watchlist in my Google account?

No. Importing copies your old Google watchlist once; after that, the extension keeps its own list. Your Google watchlist stays untouched, as a backup.

What do you store about me?

Nothing, unless you sign in to sync. If you do, the sync server stores your Google account's email and ID and your list. Nothing is sold or shared, and you can delete it all from the toolbar window. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it work on my phone?

Not yet. Phone browsers like Chrome on Android and iPhone don't run extensions. A phone version is planned.

Signing in says “Access blocked” or “This app isn't verified”

The sign-in app is new. If you see “isn't verified”, choose Continue. If it says “Access blocked”, sign-in isn't open to everyone yet. Everything else works without signing in.
