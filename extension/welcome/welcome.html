You're in. Three quick steps and your watchlist is back.

Bring Back My Google Watchlist puts the Watchlist button back on Google's movie and TV pages.

  1. Sign in with Google

    Your list is saved to your Google account, so it's the same in every browser you use. Pick the account you use on Google Search.

    If Google says the app isn't verified, click Advanced, then Continue. It's a new app.

  2. Bring over your old Google watchlist

    Your old list is still in your Google account, in your saved items.

    1. Click Open my Google saves below.
    2. Open your watchlist collection. It's often called Search Watchlist or Google TV watchlist.
    3. Scroll to the bottom so every title loads.
    4. Click Import to My Watchlist in the bottom-right corner.
    Open my Google saves

    No old watchlist? Skip this; you can start fresh.

  3. Use it like before

    • Search my watchlist on Google any time to see your list.
    • On any movie or TV show page on Google, click Add to watchlist under the title.
    • Or click the extension's icon in your toolbar. If you don't see it, click the puzzle-piece icon and pin Bring Back My Google Watchlist.
